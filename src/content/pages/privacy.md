## What this site does today

These statements are true of the site as built, and are checked automatically on
every change:

- **No cookies are set.** The site stores nothing in your browser.
- **No third-party scripts run.** No tag manager, no analytics, no embeds.
- **Fonts are served from this domain.** The three typefaces are downloaded at
  build time and served from here, not from Google Fonts, so visiting this site
  does not tell any third party that you did.
- **No JavaScript is loaded on the home page at all.**

This is why you are not being asked to accept anything.

## The newsletter

`[NEEDS SIGN-OFF]` — the sign-up form is on the home page, but the policy below
has not been agreed, and no mailing provider has been chosen.

The intended shape, which needs a decision from the organisers before it is
published as policy:

- We email you once to confirm you meant to sign up, and delete your address if
  you do not confirm.
- Two emails are promised: one when the dates are confirmed, one when the call
  for papers opens.
- Every email carries an unsubscribe link.
- What happens to the list after the event — how long it is kept, and whether it
  carries over to the next edition — is **[UNDECIDED]**.
- Whether the list is ever shared with sponsors is **[UNDECIDED]**. It should be
  stated explicitly either way.

Whoever signs this off should also confirm which provider is used and where the
data is stored, since that determines what has to be disclosed here.
