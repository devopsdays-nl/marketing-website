## The short version

This website sets no cookies, runs no third-party scripts, and does not track you.

## Fonts

The three typefaces used here are served from this domain. They are not loaded from Google Fonts or any other third party, so visiting this site does not tell anyone else that you did.

## Analytics

If we measure traffic, we use a cookieless analytics tool that records no personal data and does not follow you between sites. That is why you are not being asked to accept anything.

## The newsletter

If you sign up, we store your email address and nothing else.

- We email you once to confirm you meant to sign up. If you do not confirm, your address is deleted.
- We have promised two emails: one when the dates are confirmed, one when the call for papers opens.
- Every email has an unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing deletes your address.
- We delete the whole list after the event.
- We do not sell, share or rent the list. Sponsors do not get it.

## Ticketing and talk proposals

Tickets are handled by Tito and talk proposals by pretalx. Those are separate services with their own privacy policies, and anything you give them is covered by their terms rather than this page.

## Getting in touch

To ask what we hold about you, or to have it deleted, email [amsterdam@devopsdays.org](mailto:amsterdam@devopsdays.org).
