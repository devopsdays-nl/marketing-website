---

Going to a conference on your own is hard. Open Spaces make it harder still if you have never seen one work — you are asked to propose a session to a room full of strangers on your first morning.

Conference Buddy exists for exactly that. We pair people attending for the first time with someone who has been before. Your buddy finds you at the start, introduces you to people, explains how Open Spaces work, and makes sure you have someone to sit with at lunch.

## What a buddy does

- Meets you at the start of day one
- Explains the format, including how to propose an Open Space
- Introduces you to a few people
- Makes sure you are not eating lunch alone on the first day

## What a buddy is not

Your buddy is not a chaperone and is not responsible for you. They are someone friendly who already knows how the event works.

## Signing up

Sign-up opens with ticket sales. When you register you can ask for a buddy, or offer to be one.
